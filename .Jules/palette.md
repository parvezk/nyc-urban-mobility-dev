## 2024-08-09 - Accessible Focus States in Monochromatic UIs
**Learning:** Standard focus rings can easily get lost against dark, monochromatic backgrounds, causing accessibility issues for keyboard users. Adding a ring offset that matches the background color (`focus-visible:ring-offset-slate-900`) combined with a high-contrast ring (`focus-visible:ring-slate-400`) creates a distinct double-border effect that ensures focus is clearly visible without introducing new colors into the design system. Additionally, native `accent-color` (like `accent-blue-500`) can break strict monochromatic rules; using `accent-slate-300` keeps form controls on-brand.
**Action:** Always pair `focus-visible:ring-*` with matching `focus-visible:ring-offset-*` against dark backgrounds to guarantee contrast. Replace any default or colored `accent-*` utilities with neutral tones (e.g., `accent-slate-*`) to maintain monochromatic constraints in inputs like range sliders.
