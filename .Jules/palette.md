## 2024-05-17 - Accessible Focus States in Monochromatic UIs
**Learning:** When enforcing strict monochromatic design constraints (where data is the only source of color), standard accessible focus rings (which often rely on bright blue or high-saturation colors) conflict with the design system. Additionally, native `accent-color` (like `accent-blue-500`) used for range inputs must also be overridden.
**Action:** Use specific, high-contrast offset styles in Tailwind (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-slate-400 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-slate-900`) for interactive elements in dark-themed monochromatic UIs to guarantee visibility without introducing extraneous colors. Similarly, adjust standard UI components like range sliders to use neutral accent tones (`accent-slate-300 hover:accent-slate-200`).
