## 2026-08-07 - Monochromatic UI Constraint & Focus States
**Learning:** In a highly stylized, dark-mode visualization application where "data is the only source of color," it is crucial to adapt standard accessibility enhancements. Standard focus rings or colored accents (like `blue-500`) break the design contract and can distract from the visualization data. Icon-only buttons are particularly prone to being missed by screen readers if `aria-label` is not dynamically updated based on state (e.g., play vs. pause).
**Action:** Always verify the project's design system before applying standard UI utility classes. Use neutral tones (`slate-300`, `slate-400`) with appropriate offsets (`focus-visible:ring-offset-slate-900`) for interactive elements in monochromatic environments to maintain both aesthetic integrity and accessibility. Add dynamic ARIA labels for toggle buttons.
